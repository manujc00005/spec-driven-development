## Note on SEC-001

The sharpest of the four, and it invalidates a claim this feature made about itself.
`evidence/CONTRACT_MUTATIONS.md` records the template guard as **CAUGHT**. That is true of the
mutation that was tried — `Protocol version: \`1\`` → `\`2\``, which changes the substring — and false
of the divergence actually present: the guard is
`assertIn("Protocol version: \`1\`", template_text)`, satisfied by the field existing **anywhere, in
any spelling the core cannot parse**. `parse_fields` (`state.py:214`) is case-sensitive and does not
strip backticks, and the template states the field in the preamble rather than in `## State`, so the
core reads a template-shaped document as version 1 regardless of what it declares.

A mutation test proves a guard catches *the mutation you thought of*. It does not prove the guard
checks the property. That is worth more than the fix.

## Maintainer correction to domain:DOM-023 — the finding is right, its baseline is not

DOM-023 states that on the "converged run whose `run.jsonl` cannot be appended to" path, `main`

**It did not reach exit 70.** Reproduced by the maintainer, and independently re-run here against a
temporary extraction of `main` with `run.jsonl` occupied by a directory:

```
MAIN exit:   1
MAIN stdout: ''
MAIN stderr: ...IsADirectoryError: [Errno 21] Is a directory: '.../run.jsonl'   (raw traceback)
```

The mechanism: the first `log.emit` raises, the CLI's `except Exception` handler runs, and the
handler's **own** `log.emit("internal-error", …)` raises again — out of the handler, past every
catch, to the interpreter. `main` never got as far as printing `[INTERNAL]` or returning 70.

Why this matters rather than being a detail: DOM-023's required action asks for a decision
reconciling FR-009 and AC-008 with the tree, and that decision has to name what the baseline
actually was. Recording it as "exit 70" would authorise a difference that does not exist and leave
the real one — a traceback and exit 1, the shape a scheduler cannot branch on — unauthorised and
unpinned. The decision that resolves it (**D015**) names the true baseline, and the
`audit-unavailable` golden transcript pins the replacement.

**D012 does not authorise the difference either.** It asserts the new reporting is "what the
baseline did", which is false of this shape, and it reconciles neither FR-009 nor AC-008. D015
supersedes that claim; D012 is kept in full.
